WHAT USUALLY HAPPENS

The notes are right and the fingers are right, and yet the note comes out thin, breathy, squeaky or wobbly. Most people blow harder, because that feels like the obvious fix.

It usually makes things worse. Then they watch a video that moves too fast, or blame the whistle, and start to think a clean tone is something other people are born with.

WHY THAT IS THE WRONG STARTING POINT

Most whistle problems are sound problems, and sound problems come from a short list of causes. A whistle is sensitive to small changes in air pressure, and a steady, gentle stream almost always sounds better than a forceful one. Once you know what to check, fixing your tone is a method, not luck.
